§ 01 — Current Issue

Now我此刻正在做的事情

Not a résumé. A working list of what is currently occupying my attention, updated whenever the issue changes.

Static application security testing: reading other people's code for a living, finding the places where intent and implementation diverge, and deciding which findings are actually worth a developer's afternoon.

大部分工作不是"发现漏洞"，而是让风险变成一个可以被工程团队理解和执行的句子。

Tools · scanning · triage · developer conversations

Living in the gap between what a policy says and what actually happens on a Tuesday afternoon. Rules, evidence, process design, and the slow work of making controls feel less like theatre.

法律训练留给我的习惯：不追问"有没有写"，而追问"谁在什么时候、以什么方式执行它"。

Policy ↔ practice · evidence · coordination

A research project on AI-assisted group assignments: what happens to authorship, free-riding, and trust when everyone on the team has the same silent collaborator.

有意思的问题不是"AI 能不能写得更好"，而是当写作过程变得不透明之后，小组如何重新分配信用与责任。

Collaboration · authorship · learning

I'm in counselling. It is an ongoing practice of understanding myself with more honesty and less performance — noticing which patterns I keep describing as personality and which ones are just unfinished.

这里只写状态，不写内容。有些进展属于椅子上的五十分钟，不属于一个公开网页。

Self-work · honesty · unfinished business

§ 03 — Personal Screen Archive

Screen电影与日剧

Filed by mood, not by rating. The things I return to when a normal day needs a different shape.

  • Japanese Drama

    海女

    あまちゃん · NHK 連続テレビ小説

    2013

    A bright, strange, deeply human story about belonging, performance, and the places that keep you. 北三陸の空気を部屋の中に作る方法。

  • Japanese Drama

    重启人生

    ブラッシュアップライフ · 日本テレビ

    2023 · Written by 笨蛋节奏

    Ordinary lives, second chances, and dialogue so precisely timed it becomes music. Friendship treated as the main plot, not the subplot. 投胎のルールより、雑談の密度。

  • Japanese Drama

    热点

    ホットスポット / THE HOT SPOT · 日本テレビ

    2025 · Written by 笨蛋节奏 · St. 市川实日子

    A single mother at a business hotel at the foot of Mt. Fuji discovers her quiet colleague is an alien — and negotiates with him at the level of "please don't make it weird". Adult pragmatism as comedy. 日常の問題を、非日常の力でそっと片付ける。

  • Japanese Drama

    我的事说来话长

    俺の話は長い · 日本テレビ

    2019

    A unemployed, endlessly articulate young man defends his own life — and is gently overruled by the family who feeds him. The best show about being stuck. 言い訳の才能と、その終わり方。

  • Film

    沙丘

    Dune: Part One · Part Two

    2021 / 2024

    Resource, prophecy, and family politics at a scale that makes individual choice look irrelevant. Watching it is mostly about the silence between the sound design. システムの話としてのスペクタクル。

  • Film

    银翼杀手 2049

    Blade Runner 2049

    2017

    For the days when silence says more than dialogue. A procedural about evidence, memory, and being ordinary — which is the hardest thing to accept. 特別な存在ではない、という結末。

§ 05 — Contributors

About关于 cass 与圆圆

One editor, one supervising cat, a working list of unfinished drafts.

I work in security, but my way of thinking was shaped by finance and law.

I'm interested in how systems distribute risk, how rules become practice, and how people make decisions inside structures that were never quite designed to hold.

Outside work I write, watch Japanese dramas, revisit the same two or three films, and collect fragments of ordinary life before they lose their shape.

我现在从事安全分析，最初学的是金融和法律。它们共同决定了我看东西的方式：我关心系统如何分配风险，规则如何变成实践，以及人在一个并不完美的结构里怎样继续做决定。

工作之外，我写作、看日剧、反复看同一几部电影，和一个极度可爱的中华田园猫一起生活。这个网站是我的个人档案馆——简历的一部分，杂志的一部分，还有很多部分暂时说不清。
