Not a résumé. A working list of what is currently occupying my attention, updated whenever the issue changes.

大部分工作不是"发现漏洞"，而是让风险变成一个可以被工程团队理解和执行的句子。

Tools · scanning · triage · developer conversations

法律训练留给我的习惯：不追问"有没有写"，而追问"谁在什么时候、以什么方式执行它"。

Screen电影与日剧

Filed by mood, not by rating. The things I return to when a normal day needs a different shape.

  • Japanese Drama

    海女

    あまちゃん · NHK 連続テレビ小説

    2013

    A bright, strange, deeply human story about belonging, performance, and the places that keep you. 北三陸の空気を部屋の中に作る方法。

  • Japanese Drama

    重启人生

    ブラッシュアップライフ · 日本テレビ

    2023 · Written by 笨蛋节奏

    Ordinary lives, second chances, and dialogue so precisely timed it becomes music. Friendship treated as the main plot, not the subplot. 投胎のルールより、雑談の密度。

  • Japanese Drama

    热点

    ホットスポット / THE HOT SPOT · 日本テレビ

    2025 · Written by 笨蛋节奏 · St. 市川实日子

    A single mother at a business hotel at the foot of Mt. Fuji discovers her quiet colleague is an alien — and negotiates with him at the level of "please don't make it weird". Adult pragmatism as comedy. 日常の問題を、非日常の力でそっと片付ける。

  • Japanese Drama

    我的事说来话长

    俺の話は長い · 日本テレビ

    2019

    An unemployed, endlessly articulate young man defends his own life — and is gently overruled by the family who feeds him. The best show about being stuck. 言い訳の才能と、その終わり方。

  • Film

    沙丘

    Dune: Part One · Part Two

    2021 / 2024

    Resource, prophecy, and family politics at a scale that makes individual choice look irrelevant. Watching it is mostly about the silence between the sound design. システムの話としてのスペクタクル。

  • Film

    银翼杀手 2049

    Blade Runner 2049

    2017

    For the days when silence says more than dialogue. A procedural about evidence, memory, and being ordinary — which is the hardest thing to accept. 特別な存在ではない、という結末。

Side B · Track 05 — Contributors

About关于 cass 与钻钻

One editor, one supervising cat, a working list of unfinished drafts.

I work in application security, but my way of thinking was shaped by finance and law.

I studied Finance & Banking at Southeast University with a minor in Law, then an MSc in Business Information Systems at City University of Hong Kong. Since 2025 I've worked on static application security testing at an international bank — evaluating SAST tools across languages most people never scan, and keeping secure-development controls honest.

I'm interested in how systems distribute risk, how rules become practice, and how people make decisions inside structures that were never quite designed to hold. Outside work I write, watch Japanese dramas, and revisit the same two or three films.

本科在东南大学读金融学，辅修法学；之后在香港城市大学读商业信息系统硕士。2025 年起在一家国际银行做静态应用安全测试（SAST）：给扫描工具建基线、找覆盖盲区，也负责安全开发管控和整改跟进。再往前，在一家四大会计师事务所的法证团队实习过，用 Python 写过从上市公司公告里识别潜在舞弊事件的小程序。

金融、法律和安全，在我这里其实是同一个问题：风险被放在了哪里，谁在为它负责。工作之外，我写作、看日剧，和一只叫钻钻的中华田园猫一起生活。这个网站是我的个人档案馆——简历的一部分，杂志的一部分。
